Method

Principles behind the decisions.

01

Restraint over feature-creep

Every feature earns its place. Shipping less, and making it count, is harder than adding — and often more valuable.

02

Intelligence over automation

AI should sharpen a decision, not replace the thinking. The tools should make you more capable, not more dependent.

03

Long-term over short-term

No growth tricks, no dark patterns, no traps for attention. Built for the person who will still be here in three years.

04

Ownership over delegation

A small studio, high accountability. I would rather own a thing end to end than spread the responsibility until it disappears.

05

Clarity over cleverness

Code, design, and sentences that a person can follow. If it is clever for its own sake, it gets cut.

06

Calm over urgency

The best products do not demand attention. They earn it. Designed for focus, not for a habit.

Software that gets out of the way, respects your time, and does what it says.
