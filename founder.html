How I work

Principles that drive every decision.

01

Restraint over feature-creep

Every feature earns its place. I ship less and make it count — removing is harder than adding, and often more valuable.

02

Intelligence over automation

AI should amplify decisions, not replace thinking. I build tools that make you smarter, not ones that make you dependent.

03

Long-term over short-term

No growth hacks, no dark patterns, no engagement traps. I build for the person who'll use this in 3 years, not just today.

04

Ownership over delegation

Small team, high accountability. I'd rather own everything end-to-end than diffuse responsibility across a org chart.

05

Clarity over cleverness

Code, design, and copy that anyone can understand. If it's clever for clever's sake, it gets cut.

06

Calm over urgency

The best products don't demand your attention — they earn it. I design for focus, not for addiction.
