01 — Field

Questions first. Products when they are ready.

The studio holds two kinds of work at once: open technical studies, and systems precise enough to ship. A question is not a product. It becomes one when the idea can stand on its own.

AI, software, agents, and the tools around them are the material. The point is to build something unusual that still earns its place in daily use.

06 — Studio

An independent technology studio exploring what software can become.

XOPICX was founded in 2024 by Devk. We design and build software, AI, agents, and experimental tools — our own products, and systems for people who need software that can hold a conversation and carry the work around it.

There is no rush to scale. We own what we ship. The studio works from India, and with people elsewhere.
