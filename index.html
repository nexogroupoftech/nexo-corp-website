04 — Philosophy

NexoCorp began with a frustration: most software is loud, bloated, and built to be demonstrated. We would rather make a smaller thing that holds up. Curiosity is the brief. Restraint is how we keep it honest.

06 — About

An independent studio, built around questions.

NexoCorp was founded in 2024 by Devk. We design and build intelligent tools — our own products, and agents for people who need software that can hold a conversation and carry the work around it.

There is no rush to scale. We own what we ship. The studio works from India, and with people elsewhere.
