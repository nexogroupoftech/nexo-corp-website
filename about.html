About

NexoCorp is an independent technology studio. We build clean, intelligent products — and we stay small enough to own them.

The studio

Technology that respects your intelligence.

NexoCorp was founded with a clear intent: build technology that respects intelligence, time, and long-term thinking. The studio operates with restraint, focus, and personal ownership. There is no rush to scale — only a commitment to quality and clarity.

It did not start with a business plan. It started with a frustration — that most software is bloated, unintelligent, and built for demos rather than real use. Devk set out to fix that, one product at a time.
