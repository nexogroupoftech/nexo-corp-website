An independent technology studio exploring what software can become.

XOPICX builds software, AI, agents, and experimental tools. The studio stays small enough to own them.

The studio

Technology that respects attention.

XOPICX was founded with a clear intent: build technology that respects intelligence, time, and long-term thinking. The studio operates with restraint, focus, and personal ownership. There is no rush to scale — only a commitment to quality and clarity.

It did not start with a business plan. It started with a frustration — that most software is bloated, unintelligent, and built for demos rather than real use. Devk set out to work on that, one product at a time.
